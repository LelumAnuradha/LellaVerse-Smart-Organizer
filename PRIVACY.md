# Privacy Policy - LellaVerse Smart Organizer

**Short version: the app does not collect, send or share any data.**

- The app works completely offline. It has no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
- It only reads and moves files inside the folders that **you** add to the app.
- It saves two small files in your user folder: a settings file and a history file
  (used for the Undo feature). They never leave your PC. You can delete them any time.
- Virus scanning uses the Windows Defender that is already installed on your PC.
  The app itself does not send your files anywhere.
- If you enable "Start with Windows", the app adds a start-up entry for your user only.
  You can switch it off in the app's Settings tab.

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
